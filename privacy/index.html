VibePlay Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

VibePlay does not collect, sell or share your personal information. Everything it saves stays on your Roku device, and it only connects to the services you choose to use.

Information stored on your Roku

VibePlay saves a few settings in your Roku device's own storage so you don't have to enter them every time. This information never leaves your device, except to sign in to the provider you chose (see the next section).

While the app is open, VibePlay also keeps temporary copies of channel lists and playlists to make browsing and search faster. Roku deletes these automatically when the app closes.

Information sent over the internet

VibePlay connects only to the addresses you give it, or that your provider gives it. It sends only what each service needs:

  • Your provider list address. VibePlay downloads the list from the address you type. If you type a short name, VibePlay opens tinyurl.com/vibeplay-<name>, so TinyURL receives that request.
  • Your provider's servers. To sign in, VibePlay sends your username and password to the provider you chose, and only to that provider. It then requests channel lists, program guide information and video streams from that provider's servers.
  • Built-in demo streams. If you use the demo, VibePlay plays openly licensed test videos from the companies that host them.

Every request identifies itself as "VibePlay" so services know which app is connecting. Each of these services handles your information under its own privacy policy. VibePlay is not affiliated with any provider and does not control what they collect.

What VibePlay does not do

  • It has no user accounts and runs no servers of its own, so it has nowhere to send your information.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising tools.
  • It does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
  • It does not provide, host or recommend any channels or content. You choose your own provider.

Roku's own data collection

Your Roku device and the Roku platform collect their own information, such as device and usage data. That is handled by Roku under Roku's privacy policy, not by VibePlay.

Deleting your information

  • Remove your password: on the VibePlay home screen, choose "Change provider / sign out".
  • Remove a favorite: highlight the channel in Live TV and press the ✱ button on your remote.
  • Remove everything: uninstall VibePlay from your Roku. This deletes all information the app saved on the device.

VibePlay keeps no copies anywhere else, so there is nothing further to request or delete.

Children

VibePlay is not directed at children under 18 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
